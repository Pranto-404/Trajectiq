## Deeper functional documentation

- [Eco-Metropolis](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/nasa-space-apps-2024/find-a-team/innovisionaries/?tab=project): documents three learning levels and distinct planning roles. This supports brief onboarding followed by optional deeper tools. Read project documentation; did not execute its downloadable game.
- [Connected Earth Museum](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/nasa-space-apps-2024/find-a-team/connected-earth-museum/?tab=project): documents guided navigation, data visualizations, and a knowledge mini-game, including an explicit loading-time caveat. Applied the principle of guidance before complexity, while preserving Trajectiq's bundled/offline model. Did not benchmark its runtime.

## Findings applied

- A clear first-visit guide explains goal, build, flight and debrief without assuming engineering knowledge.
- A bottom-right assistant gives contextual next steps; advanced controls are folded away and the composer remains visible.
- Advice is checked against actual battery, recorder, active run, and difficulty-specific science requirements.
- Destination-specific experiment bounds now agree with the engine; no new unverified NASA constants were introduced.
- Original aurora and meteor effects add motion while preserving hidden-tab and reduced-motion behavior.

Useful proposal signals: 나이사 describes budget/fuel/time trade-offs; -1Apollo proposes an end-to-end simulator; Adecce proposes topic units; Alienigenas sin experiencia proposes rehearsing decisions; Antennae proposes a mission sandbox. These are ideas in public descriptions, not independently verified implementations.

### Additional useful documented patterns

- 3RB / Protocol Globe: resources and staff choices tied to ecosystem survival. Reinforces consequences over decorative scores.
- 3xoplorers and 42Apex: instrument/research methods become interactive decisions; keep Trajectiq payload and data trade-offs visible.
- 3AM Zombie Insomniacs: upcoming and previous satellite acquisitions; keep contact timing visible beside recorder state.
- 404 Brain Not Found: observation and environmental decisions in a farming game; connect feedback to the player's own choices.
- 4BITS: simplified orbital visualization for beginners; distinguish schematic views from flight trajectories.
- 405 Found: separate analytics, explanation and quiz; use optional tools instead of presenting all controls upfront.

No external project code/assets were incorporated. Current changes retain cited NASA-derived physical constants and educational scenario assumptions; no live NASA feed is claimed.
